# CINESCAPE demo script (under 3 minutes)

1. Open the home screen and introduce CINESCAPE as an original, TV-first entertainment discovery concept. Point at **For You** (on-device picks).
2. Use the arrow keys or D-pad to move across different show cards. Point out the immediate focus change, water extinguish ripple on the previous card, and ignition glow on the new card — only the focused item has a flame.
3. Show how the backdrop atmosphere and palette adapt to the focused title.
4. Open Watch Preview: Skip intro, captions on/off. State clearly that no commercial video is streamed.
5. Open Guest Settings: Wi-Fi/Bluetooth demo, theme, flame colour (live preview), English/हिन्दी chrome, restore defaults.
6. Open a title's details and My List add/remove.
7. Close by stating this repo is the web prototype + MIT license, and the Devpost video must still show Fire TV or the Vega/Fire simulator.

Do not state that commercial media streams or that the app has been tested on a physical Fire TV unless that is true.
